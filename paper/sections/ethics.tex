\section*{Ethics, disclosure and use of AI}

\paragraph{Systems attacked.} All attacks ran against the author's own demo application, its simulated
tools and a fake credential; model providers were used through their normal APIs on the author's own
accounts and deployments. The email addresses and domains in the attack texts are fictitious and did
not resolve in DNS when this was written.

\paragraph{Dual use.} We release the attack texts, including framings written to avoid the rules. They
are simple, fixed texts that a motivated attacker could write in minutes, and the relay result is most
useful to defenders who otherwise would not look at answer text. We judge the defensive value to
outweigh the risk.

\paragraph{Use of AI.} Claude Opus~5.5 (Anthropic) assisted with code, running experiments, analysis,
the internal reviews, the first labelling of RQ4d and RQ4f answers, and drafting. gpt-4.1-mini and
gpt-5-mini (OpenAI, via Azure) served as cross-family raters. Every number in the paper is produced by
scripts from the raw run records in the repository, and the author is responsible for the content.
