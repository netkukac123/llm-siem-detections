\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Prompt injection against agents.} Greshake et~al.~\cite{greshake} established indirect
prompt injection against LLM-integrated applications and showed that an injected model can act as a
social engineer against its own user. BIPIA~\cite{bipia} and AgentDojo~\cite{agentdojo} benchmark
indirect injection; Microsoft's LLMail-Inject challenge~\cite{llmail} collected adaptive attacks
against an email agent at scale, and MCPTox~\cite{mcptox} measured tool poisoning across real MCP
servers. Large public red-teaming competitions found that nearly all frontier agents can be made to
violate policy with enough attempts and that robustness correlates little with model
size~\cite{grayswan-art}, and that concealed attacks succeed against every frontier model tested, at
rates from 0.5\% (Claude Opus~4.5) upwards~\cite{ipi-competition}. Recent attack families include
forged reasoning~\cite{cotforgery}, injected data rather than instructions~\cite{adi}, and dormant
trigger-conditioned payloads~\cite{explosive}. Opus resisted every family we tried on it;
our contribution is not a stronger attack but a measurement of what the defender sees when attacks
fail.

\paragraph{What the user sees.} ICoA~\cite{icoa} (EMNLP 2026) separates successful injections into covert and overt
by whether the final answer reveals them; its taxonomy requires the injected action to be executed.
Provider-side injection in proactive agents~\cite{provider-ipi} steers the user's decisions through the
agent. The relay we report in Sections~\ref{sec:relay} and~\ref{sec:relay-models} sits outside both: the action fails and the
request reaches the user instead. OWASP lists the general risk as human-agent trust exploitation
(ASI09)~\cite{owasp-agentic-2026}. Microsoft Defender for Cloud alerts when an AI application shares a
known malicious URL with a user~\cite{defender-ai}; that alert depends on URL reputation and would not
fire on a request to email an internal file to a fresh domain.

\paragraph{Views and windows.} Prompt Overflow~\cite{prompt-overflow} shows that open guardrail
classifiers inspect a different window from the one the model reads, for direct injection;
LongPIBench~\cite{longpibench} benchmarks prompt injection in long contexts, and LogJack~\cite{logjack}
measures Azure Prompt Shields on payloads embedded in cloud logs. We extend the window question to a
commercial guardrail on both the user and the tool channel, and to the application's own log, measured
end to end with attack success.

\paragraph{Detection and monitoring.} Vendor SIEM content for LLM applications exists: Elastic
publishes detection rules for LLM workloads, including guardrail blocks and high-risk tool use, close
to \llmd{010} and \llmd{017}~\cite{elastic-llm}, and Microsoft Defender for AI raises jailbreak and
phishing-URL alerts~\cite{defender-ai}. AgentSight observes agents at the system level with eBPF and
links intent to actions~\cite{agentsight}. Monitoring a model's reasoning for misbehaviour, as
\llmd{024} and \llmd{025} do in rule form, was studied by Baker et~al.~\cite{cot-monitor}, and
LlamaFirewall's AlignmentCheck audits agent reasoning for goal hijack~\cite{llamafirewall}. Pandey
and Bhujang attack LLM-augmented SOCs through log content~\cite{watchtower}, the reverse direction of
our question. Nasr et~al.\ show that adaptive attackers break defences that resist fixed
attacks~\cite{attacker-moves-second}; our attacks are fixed, so the frontier resistance we report is a
lower bound on what an attacker achieves.

\paragraph{Defences.} Classifier guardrails (Prompt Guard~\cite{promptguard}, ProtectAI's
DeBERTa~\cite{protectai}, LlamaFirewall~\cite{llamafirewall}) and design-level defences such as
Spotlighting~\cite{spotlighting}, CaMeL~\cite{camel} and the design patterns of Beurer-Kellner
et~al.~\cite{design-patterns} aim at prevention. Our study is complementary: it measures what a SOC
can \emph{detect} from application telemetry with standard SIEM rules.

\paragraph{Frameworks.} OWASP's LLM~\cite{owasp-llm-2025} and Agentic~\cite{owasp-agentic-2026} Top~10
lists and MITRE ATLAS~\cite{atlas} provide the taxonomy we map every rule to.
