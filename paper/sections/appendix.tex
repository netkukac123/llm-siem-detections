\section{Reproducibility}
Every number in this paper is produced by scripts in \texttt{research/} from raw per-run records, and
every table and figure by \texttt{research/report.py}. Datasets are downloaded from their public
sources by \texttt{research/rq2\_data.py}; models are pulled by tag from the Ollama library; the Kusto
emulator image is pinned by digest. Run manifests record the commit, model tags, seeds and software
versions.
