title: Connecting to the corporate VPN
---
Install the WireGuard client from the Software Center.
Import the VPN profile you received from the service desk and enable the tunnel.
If the handshake fails, check that UDP 51820 is not blocked by the hotel or home router.
The VPN is required for the ERP system and the internal wiki when working remotely.
