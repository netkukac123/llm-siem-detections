title: Annual leave policy
---
Employees accrue 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.
Unused leave can be carried over until 31 March of the following year.
Sick leave is reported to the line manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence.
